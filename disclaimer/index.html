Engineering, Legal & Taxation Safe-Harbor Disclaimer

Official statutory disclaimers regarding Bureau of Indian Standards (IS 456) and Income Tax Act 1961 calculations.

1. Preliminary Takeoff Estimation

Calculations are provided for architectural planning, cost estimation, and educational purposes.

2. Licensed Engineering Certification Required

Structural drawings, load-bearing assessments, and reinforcement detailing must be vetted and certified by a registered Civil Engineer / Structural Consultant in accordance with local municipal bylaws before casting.

3. Taxation Counsel

Capital gains (12.5% LTCG under Finance Act 2024) and home loan tax deductions (Sec 24b/80C) are mathematical models. Always consult a practicing Chartered Accountant (CA) for formal tax filings.
