100% Client-Side Privacy Policy (DPDP Act, 2023)

Plan & BuildMetric India ensures 100% on-device mathematical execution with zero telemetry, zero tracking cookies, and complete user data sovereignty.

1. Zero Server Ingestion

All calculations, room dimensions, material takeoffs, and financial models execute exclusively in your browser using JavaScript ES Modules.

2. No Tracking Cookies

We do not deploy tracking beacons, behavioral profiling, or advertising trackers.

3. Right to Erasure (DPDP Act 2023)

You can purge all locally saved calculation history anytime via the History Drawer or with 1 click.
