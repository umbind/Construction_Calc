⚖️ Engineering, Legal & Taxation Disclaimer

1. Preliminary Estimation Safe Harbor: All calculations, material quantities, mix ratios, and cost projections generated by Plan & BuildMetric are provided solely for preliminary takeoff estimation, architectural planning, and educational purposes.

2. Structural & Engineering Certification: Structural drawings, load-bearing assessments, soil bearing capacities, and reinforcement detailing must be certified and vetted by a licensed Structural Engineer or Registered Civil Engineer in accordance with Bureau of Indian Standards (IS 456:2000, IS 1786, IS 2185) and local municipal building bylaws before site procurement or casting.

3. Taxation & Legal Disclosures: Financial analytics (including 12.5% Long-Term Capital Gains Tax under the Finance Act 2024, and Section 24b/80C Home Loan tax deductions under the Income Tax Act, 1961) represent generalized mathematical estimates. Users must consult a qualified Chartered Accountant (CA) or legal professional for formal tax filings and transaction due diligence.

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy Policy (DPDP Act, 2023)

1. Zero Server-Side Data Ingestion: Plan & BuildMetric is engineered with an air-gapped, client-side execution architecture. All calculation inputs, dimensions, property valuations, and bill of quantities are processed exclusively in your device's browser using JavaScript ES Modules.

2. No Tracking or Profiling: We do not deploy behavioral tracking cookies, canvas fingerprinting, or third-party data broker analytics.

3. DPDP Act (2023) & Right to Erasure: Any saved calculation history is stored strictly within your browser's sandboxed localStorage. You have absolute data sovereignty and can delete all stored history anytime via the History Drawer or with 1 click below.
