## 1. Quick Overview & Key Highlights

- **100% Free & No Login Required**: Access all tools instantly with zero registration or paywalls.
- **100% Private (On-Device)**: Your project dimensions, room sizes, and financial numbers are calculated purely within your browser. Nothing is ever sent to a remote database.
- **Indian Standard Compliant**: Formulas are directly aligned with Bureau of Indian Standards (**IS 456:2000**, **IS 1786**, **IS 2185**), National Building Code (**NBC 2016**), and **Budget 2024** tax rules.
- **Works Offline**: Install as a mobile or desktop app for instant on-site construction calculations without internet.

## 4. Step-by-Step Guide for Each Calculator

### 1. 🧱 Concrete & 50kg Cement Bags (IS 456:2000)
- **Use Case**: Estimating RCC roof slabs, plinth beams, column footings, or round pile foundations.
- **Steps**:
  1. Select shape: **Slab / Beam / Footing** (Rectangular) or **Round Column / Pile** (Cylindrical).
  2. Adjust Length, Width, and Thickness (in inches).
  3. Choose the **IS 456 Mix Grade**:
     - `M20 (1 : 1.5 : 3)` — Standard RCC slabs, beams & columns.
     - `M25 (1 : 1 : 2)` — Heavy load foundations & commercial columns.
     - `M15 (1 : 2 : 4)` — Pathways, flooring PCC bed.
     - `M7.5 (1 : 4 : 8)` — Plain cement concrete sub-base.
  4. Adjust local unit prices for Cement (₹/bag), M-Sand (₹/Brass), and Aggregate/Gitti (₹/Brass).
- **Result**: Wet/Dry volume in $m^3$ and Brass, exact number of **50kg Cement Bags**, sand volume in Brass, and estimated material cost.

---

### 2. 📐 Drywall, False Ceiling & Cement Plaster
- **Use Case**: Estimating Gyproc gypsum ceiling boards or 12mm/20mm internal/external wall plastering.
- **Steps**:
  1. Toggle between **Gyproc Gypsum Boards** or **Cement Plaster**.
  2. For Plaster: enter wall Length and Height, select plaster thickness (12mm single coat vs 20mm double coat) and mix ratio (1:4 vs 1:6).
  3. For Gypsum: enter room dimensions and board unit price.
- **Result**: Number of 6x4 ft gypsum sheets, jointing compound bags, or 50kg cement bags and sand required.

---

### 3. 🪨 Vitrified Floor & Wall Tiles
- **Use Case**: Estimating living room floor tiles, bathroom wall dado, or anti-skid terrace tiles.
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter Room Length and Width (in feet).
  2. Select Indian tile format:
     - `600 x 1200 mm (2x4 ft GVT Slab)`
     - `600 x 600 mm (2x2 ft Vitrified)`
     - `1200 x 1200 mm (4x4 ft Large Format)`
     - `300 x 450 mm (Wall Dado)`
     - `300 x 300 mm (Anti-Skid)`
  3. Toggle **4" Skirting Tiles** checkbox if perimeter skirting is needed.
  4. Set tile price (₹/sq.ft), adhesive price (₹/20kg bag), and grout price (₹/kg).
- **Result**: Total carpet area, exact number of **Tile Boxes needed**, 20kg polymer adhesive bags, and epoxy grout in kg.

---

### 4. 🏗️ Brickwork Masonry & TMT Steel (IS 1786)
- **Use Case**: Estimating red clay bricks, AAC lightweight blocks, or Fe 500D TMT reinforcement steel.
- **Steps**:
  1. Select wall type: `9" External Red Brick Wall`, `4.5" Internal Partition`, `AAC Lightweight Blocks`, or `RCC Slab Fe 500D TMT Steel`.
  2. Enter wall length, height, and subtract door/window openings.
  3. For RCC steel: enter slab area and steel density ratio (standard $80	ext{ kg/m}^3$).
- **Result**: Number of modular bricks/blocks, cement bags for mortar, or total **TMT Steel weight in kg and Tonnes**.

---

### 5. 🎨 Interior/Exterior Paint & Wall Putty
- **Use Case**: Fresh 2-coat painting or repaint maintenance for apartments and independent houses.
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter total wall surface area and ceiling area (in sq.ft).
  2. Select number of coats (1, 2, or 3).
  3. Enable/disable **40kg Wall Putty Bags** and **Acrylic Primer**.
- **Result**: Total paint needed in **Litres**, 40kg wall putty bags, primer litres, and combined cost.

---

### 6. 🏠 Roof Slab Casting & Waterproofing
- **Use Case**: RCC roof casting, terrace waterproofing, or industrial profile sheet roofing.
- **Steps**:
  1. Select mode: `RCC Roof Slab Casting`, `Terrace Waterproofing (Dr. Fixit Liquid Membrane)`, or `Industrial Profile Sheets (JSW Colouron+)`.
  2. Set terrace area and material prices.
- **Result**: Waterproofing chemical in Litres, profile sheets count with self-drilling screws, or concrete casting takeoff.

---

### 7. 📈 Rental Yield & Cap Rate
- **Use Case**: Analyzing residential or commercial rental return on investment (ROI).
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter Property Purchase / Market Value (₹).
  2. Enter Monthly Rental Income (₹).
  3. Input annual expenses: Society Maintenance, Municipal Property Tax, and Insurance.
  4. Set Vacancy Buffer (e.g. 4%).
- **Result**: **Gross Rental Yield (%)**, **Net Cap Rate (%)**, Annual Net Operating Income (NOI), and payback period in years.

---

### 8. 🏦 Home Loan EMI & Tax Benefits
- **Use Case**: Calculating monthly home loan installments and income tax savings under the Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter Property Cost and Loan Borrowing Amount (₹).
  2. Input Annual Interest Rate (e.g., 8.5%) and Tenure in Years (e.g., 20 years).
  3. Select your Income Tax Slab (10%, 20%, or 30%).
- **Result**: Monthly EMI, Total Interest Payable, **Section 24b Tax Savings (₹2,00,000 max rebate)**, and **Section 80C Principal Tax Savings**.

---

### 9. 🔄 Property Resale & 12.5% LTCG Tax (Budget 2024)
- **Use Case**: Computing net profit after selling real estate under the revised **Finance Act 2024** tax regime.
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter Original Purchase Price and Resale Selling Price (₹).
  2. Enter Renovation/Improvement Cost and Holding Period in Months.
  3. Set State Stamp Duty (%) and Brokerage/Legal Fee (%).
- **Result**: **12.5% Long-Term Capital Gains Tax (without indexation)**, Stamp Duty cost, Brokerage fees, and Net Realized Take-Home Profit.

---

### 10. 💰 Construction Loan & Draw Schedule
- **Use Case**: Managing short-term construction financing and milestone-based draw schedules.
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter Total Credit Facility (₹).
  2. Input Annual Interest Rate (e.g., 12.5%), Tenure in Months, and Processing Fee (%).
- **Result**: Monthly Draw Interest, Total Financing Cost, Processing Fee, and Net Disbursed Funds.

---

### 11. ❄️ HVAC AC Cooling Tonnage & Power
- **Use Case**: Sizing air conditioning split/cassette units per National Building Code (NBC 2016).
- **Steps**:
  1. Enter Room Length, Width, and Ceiling Height (in feet).
  2. Select Indian Climate Zone:
     - *Warm & Humid* (Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Kochi)
     - *Hot & Dry* (Delhi-NCR, Rajasthan, Ahmedabad)
     - *Composite* (UP, Bihar, Punjab, MP)
     - *Temperate* (Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad)
     - *Cold* (Shimla, Srinagar)
  3. Check if room is on the **Top Floor / Roof exposed** or has large glass windows.
  4. Enter daily usage hours and local electricity tariff (₹/Unit).
- **Result**: Recommended AC Tonnage (e.g. `1.5 TR Split AC`), cooling capacity in BTU/hr, and **Estimated Monthly Electricity Bill (₹)**.

- **📋 Copy Summary**: Tap the **`Copy`** button at the top of any active calculator. A formatted text summary with all key metrics will be copied to your clipboard ready to paste into WhatsApp, email, or client proposals.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: What is a "Brass" in Indian construction?
> **Answer**: In Indian construction, **1 Brass** is equal to **100 Cubic Feet (cu.ft / CFT)**, which is approximately **$2.8317	ext{ m}^3$**. It is the standard wholesale trade unit for sand, aggregate (gitti), and earthwork excavation.

#### Q2: How many cement bags are in 1 cubic meter of M20 concrete?
> **Answer**: Per IS 456:2000 nominal mix ratios with standard 1.54 dry volume factor, $1	ext{ m}^3$ of M20 concrete requires approximately **8.1 to 8.2 standard 50kg cement bags**.

#### Q3: Does this site save my property or pricing data?
> **Answer**: **No.** All mathematical computations execute exclusively in your browser's JavaScript sandbox. No project data or financial information is transmitted to any server.

#### Q4: Are the calculations legally certified?
> **Answer**: All calculations provide preliminary engineering and cost estimates. Structural load designs and reinforcement schedules must be stamped and certified by a registered Civil Engineer / Structural Consultant in accordance with local municipal bylaws.
