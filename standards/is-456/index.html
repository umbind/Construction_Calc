IS 456:2000 Concrete Mix Ratios, Dry Volume & Cement Bags

Technical summary and mathematical formulations for Bureau of Indian Standards IS 456:2000 concrete mix design.

📐 Nominal Mix Proportions (IS 456:2000)

  • M20 (1 : 1.5 : 3): Standard RCC for slabs, beams, and columns (Target characteristic compressive strength: 20 N/mm² at 28 days).
  • M25 (1 : 1 : 2): Heavy load foundations, commercial pillars, and water retaining structures.
  • M15 (1 : 2 : 4): Flooring bed, pathways, plinth base, and boundary walls.
  • M7.5 (1 : 4 : 8): Plain Cement Concrete (PCC) foundation sub-base.

⚡ Dry Volume Conversion Factor

When mixing dry cement, sand, and aggregate with water, the volume decreases due to void filling. Per standard Indian civil engineering practice, a multiplier of 1.54 is applied to wet volume to calculate dry material quantities.

Dry Volume = Wet Volume (m³) × 1.54
